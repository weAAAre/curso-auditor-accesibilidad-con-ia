# Source and attribution

This teaching site is a presentation-only copy of the **inaccessible (“before”)**
pages from the W3C WAI [Before and After Demonstration
(BAD)](https://www.w3.org/WAI/demos/bad/).

The W3C teaching wrapper and links to annotations, reports, and the accessible
comparison have been removed so learners can investigate the site without
solution hints. The CityLights page content and deliberate accessibility issues
remain unchanged, apart from relative navigation needed for static hosting.
